How it works

Five passes, start to finish.

What actually happens in your house, in order. Nothing skipped to save time.

  1. 01

    Step 1: Walk the room

    We look at the floor before we quote: the fibre, the traffic lanes, and what the marks really are.

  2. 02

    Step 2: Treat the marks

    Every mark gets its own pre-treatment, left for ten minutes before any machine touches it.

  3. 03

    Step 3: Pull it out

    Brushes loosen the soil, then water at around 230°F rinses it out and back to the truck.

  4. 04

    Step 4: Set the pile

    A grooming rake stands the fibres up, so the carpet dries upright instead of matted flat.

  5. 05

    Step 5: Leave it drying

    Air movers stay running. Walk on it in about two hours; fully dry in four to six.

What usually happens

Three typical jobs,
and what to expect.

A pet accident in a rental, before the walkthrough

The situation
The walkthrough is on Friday, and there is a pet accident in the back bedroom that has been there longer than anyone wants to say.
What we do
Find every affected area, not just the visible one, and treat each separately before the room is extracted. Priced per area, so you know the number first.
What to expect
Most marks lift completely. An older accident that reached the pad can leave a shadow, and we will tell you on the walk-through if that is likely.

A sectional two kids grew up on

The situation
The seat cushions are darker than the arms, and one corner has a long history of juice.
What we do
Read the fabric code, test an area nobody sees, then pre-treat the seats and arms before extracting the whole piece.
What to expect
The colour usually evens out. Some old stains have set into the fibre and will lighten rather than disappear, and we say which before we start.

Grout that went grey and looks like it needs replacing

The situation
Kitchen tile where the grout has gone from light to grey, and the whole floor looks tired enough to rip out.
What we do
High-pressure cleaning works the grout lines and recovers the dirty water as it goes, instead of leaving it to settle back in.
What to expect
Often the grout comes back close to its original colour. Cracked or missing grout is a repair, not a clean, and we will say so.

FAQ

Straight answers.

Something else? Call (951) 567-3513 and ask.

What does it cost?

Carpet is $45 a room with a three-room minimum, and every visit has a $135 minimum service call. The full list is in Prices, and we confirm the total on the walk-through before any work starts.

How long until I can walk on it?

About two hours. It is fully dry in four to six, and we leave air movers running to get it there.

Will the marks come back?

Sometimes a spill that went deep into the pile wicks back up as the carpet dries. That is what the 14-day redo is for: if a spot comes back within 14 days, we come back and redo it, at no charge.

Do I need to move the furniture?

Clear the small things off the floor — shoes, toys, anything fragile — and we will talk through the larger pieces on the walk-through.

What about pets and kids?

Extraction is a rinse. The pre-treatment and the soil are flushed out of the pile and pulled back to the truck, rather than left sitting in the carpet. It is walkable in about two hours and dry in four to six, and keeping pets and kids off it until then is the simplest approach. If you have a question about a particular product, ask us on the walk-through.

Is it worth cleaning, or should I replace it?

Walk the room with us. Worn-through backing, carpet coming away from its backing, and pile crushed flat in the traffic lanes do not come back with cleaning, and we will say so. Soil and most stains usually do. If it is not worth cleaning, we will not sell you a clean.
